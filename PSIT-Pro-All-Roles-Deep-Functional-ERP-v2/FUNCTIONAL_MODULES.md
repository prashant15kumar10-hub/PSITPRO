# Functional role modules

The previous `/portal/[module]` route displayed the same four placeholder boxes for every category. It has been replaced with a real, database-backed operational workspace.

## Coverage

- 19 tailored role experiences
- 187 configured navigation links
- 125 role-module links served by the functional workspace
- 62 links served by existing specialized PSIT Pro pages
- 123 unique database-backed modules

## Every generic module now includes

- Purpose-specific terminology, statuses, categories and quick actions
- Summary cards calculated from database records
- Search and status filters
- Paginated data table
- Create-record form
- Status-update controls
- Personal record archiving
- Due dates, priorities, references, contacts, locations, amounts and metrics
- Status-distribution analytics
- Recent activity history
- CSV export
- Server-side role and record visibility checks
- Useful fictional demo records immediately after setup

## Purpose-built domain families

- Academics: attendance, assignments, examinations, results, timetable and research
- Library: catalogue, members, issue/return, reservations, fines and inventory
- Placement: companies, jobs, drives, eligibility, interviews, offers and internships
- Hostel: rooms, allocations, outpass, mess, visitors, complaints and fees
- Transport: vehicles, drivers, routes, stops, allocation and maintenance
- Finance: fees, salary, expenses, income, scholarships, refunds, invoices and budgets
- Medical: appointments, visits, emergencies, medicines and restricted records
- Laboratories: practicals, equipment, allocation, maintenance and consumables
- Procurement: inventory, vendors, requests, quotations, orders and goods received
- IT: users, access, authentication, devices, integrations, backups, logs and security
- Alumni: profiles, networking, mentoring, events, referrals and donations
- Management: teams, approvals, workload, department planning and progress

Run `npm run setup` after extracting this version. Setup creates the new portal tables and recreates the fictional demo dataset.
